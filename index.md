## About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Uppsala University, working in
mathematical physics. My research concerns the structure of supersymmetric quantum field theories:
the geometry of Coulomb branches of four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ superconformal field theories,
generalised symmetries, and the geometric constructions that give rise to these theories.

Before returning to Uppsala I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Center for Mathematical Sciences and
Applications at Harvard University (2024–2025), and a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of
Physics at the University of Cincinnati (2023–2024). I completed my PhD at Uppsala University in 2023
under the supervision of Michele Del Zotto, after an MSc in Particles, Strings and Cosmology at Durham
University and an MA in Mathematics at the University of Cambridge. The full details are on the
[CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) page, and the courses I have taught are listed
under [Teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).

## Research interests

- Coulomb branch geometry of four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ SCFTs
- Generalised symmetries, symmetry TFTs and anomalies
- Geometric engineering, orbifolds and quivers

More detail and a full list of publications are on the [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page.
